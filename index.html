One record for the whole job.

Field, superintendent, VDC and owner work from the same as-built data: what was installed, where and when. Fewer RFIs, faster approvals.

  • Safety assurance

    Flags safety-critical objects near the trench and keeps a time-stamped record of the work, supporting liability and insurance claims.
